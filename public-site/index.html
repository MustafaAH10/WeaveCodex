Simple by design

A control loop you can explain.

  1. 01

    Describe the outcome

    Type it or say it out loud. Start from a blank canvas or a saved workflow.

  2. 02

    Draw the important moments

    Add Codex turns, exact checks, human decisions, and arrows. Nothing else is forced on you.

  3. 03

    Run, see, and reuse

    Follow progress step by step, inspect evidence, then save the loop for the next task.

Open source and local

Your workflow should belong to you.

WeaveCodex uses the official local Codex app-server and your existing ChatGPT/Codex sign-in. It adds a human-owned workflow layer without replacing Codex’s tools, sandbox, or agent loop.
