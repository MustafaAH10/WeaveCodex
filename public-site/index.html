Three useful workflows

Built around the decisions
people actually care about.

Finance analyst

Turn a messy workbook into a board-ready view.

Pause on assumptions. Require the totals to reconcile before the dashboard is delivered.

  1. 01Inspect workbook
  2. 02Clean rows
  3. 03Approve assumptions
  4. 04Build dashboard
  5. 05Reconcile totals
  6. 06Write summary
Frontend designer

Explore before committing to a direction.

Compare two concepts, choose the stronger one, then require accessibility checks.

  1. 01Audit the page
  2. 02Sketch two routes
  3. 03Choose direction
  4. 04Implement
  5. 05Test accessibility
  6. 06Visual review
Engineering lead

Control a risky repair without slowing it down.

Make the diagnosis and repair plan visible before Codex changes the repository.

  1. 01Collect evidence
  2. 02Reproduce issue
  3. 03Approve repair
  4. 04Patch safely
  5. 05Run regression
  6. 06Write incident note
