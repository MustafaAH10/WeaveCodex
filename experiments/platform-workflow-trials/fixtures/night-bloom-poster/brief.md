- Event name: **Night Bloom**
- Date: **17 October**
- Venue: **Riverside Makers Hall**
- Admission: **Free entry**
- Palette: ink `#101828`, moon `#F8F5EC`, orchid `#8B5CF6`, leaf `#2E7D5B`.
- Direction: quiet editorial typography with an abstract botanical motif; the event name is the
  dominant element, followed by date and venue.
- Accessibility: include an SVG `<title>` and `<desc>`, maintain readable text sizes, and explain
  the hierarchy and accessibility decisions in `design-notes.md`.
- Constraints: no external images, fonts, scripts, or network assets.
